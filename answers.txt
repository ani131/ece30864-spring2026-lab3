Pros
Super Flexible: You can change project goals anytime without ruining everything.
Early Previews: You see working parts early instead of waiting until the end.

Cons
No Set Deadline: It is hard to know the final cost or finish date.
Too Many Meetings: Daily check-ins and constant talking take up a lot of time.

2. 
The Scrum Master protects the team's process, while the Product Owner decides what features the team actually builds.

3.
The three main meetings during a sprint are planning at the start, check-ins during the week, and reviews at the end.

4. 
An Epic represents a massive project goal that is broken down into feature-focused Stories for users, technical Tasks for developers to complete, and Bugs to fix broken code.
